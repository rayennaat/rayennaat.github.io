Sweet Recipes is a cute and user-friendly recipe viewer website designed to display dessert and drink recipes in a beautiful way.

The project uses a soft pink theme, image-based recipe cards, and simple category filters to make browsing recipes feel clean and enjoyable.

> [!NOTE] Live Demo
> You can visit the project here: [Sweet Recipes](https://rayennaat.github.io/sweet-recipes)

## Recipe Browsing

The website lets users explore different sweet recipes through categories such as cakes, cookies, and drinks.

Each recipe card is designed to be easy to scan, with a clear image and recipe title so users can quickly find something they want to prepare.

![Sweet Recipes category filters and recipe cards](<./images/Sweet Recipes/pic2.png>)

## Detailed Recipe Popup

When a user clicks on a recipe card, the website opens a detailed recipe popup.

This popup shows the ingredients and step-by-step instructions, giving users everything they need without forcing them to leave the page or search through long content.

![Sweet Recipes detailed recipe popup with ingredients and instructions](<./images/Sweet Recipes/pic3.png>)

## Why This Project Matters

This project focuses on clean design, simple navigation, and an enjoyable user experience.

It is suitable for cooking blogs, personal recipe collections, beginner-friendly food websites, or any project that needs a friendly way to present recipes with images and clear instructions.
